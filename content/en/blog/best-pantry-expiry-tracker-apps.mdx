The best pantry inventory app is the one you'll actually keep using, and for most people that comes down to a few practical differences rather than the feature checklist. The expiry trackers below all handle the core job; where they split is on price model, cloud versus local storage, and whether they make you create an account. This comparison marks the gaps rather than guessing at them.

## What to look for

The useful features are the same across the category: **barcode scanning** to add items fast, **best-before reminders**, and some way to organise **fridge, freezer and pantry**. The real differences are the **pricing model** (free, freemium, one-time or subscription) and whether the app is **cloud-based with an account** or **local and offline**.

## The apps, compared

| App | What it does | Price | Platforms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Expiroo** | Barcode, no account, offline | One-time purchase | Android |
| Fridgely | Barcode + receipt scanning, alerts, recipes, spaces, family, sync | Freemium (Premium adds CSV export) | iOS + Android |
| Pantry Check | Barcode, cloud inventory with sync, best-before reminders, lists, family sharing | Free to 200 items; Premium $11.99 / Pro $29.99 (as of July 2026) | iOS + Android |
| NoWaste | Fridge/freezer/pantry inventories, barcode + AI, sync | Freemium (Pro annual + Lifetime) | iOS + Android |
| Best Before | Per-item dates, barcode, optional account for sync | One-time (iOS) / free (Android) | iOS + Android |

Two details we couldn't fully confirm: for **Pantry Check**, whether it works entirely offline or needs an account isn't clear from its listing; the cloud sync points to an account, but we're not stating it as fact. For **Fridgely**, only the "offline receipt scanner" is confirmed to work offline.

## Where Expiroo fits

Most of these apps lean on the cloud, a login, and a freemium or subscription upsell. [Expiroo](/en) makes the opposite trade on purpose: your inventory stays in a local database on your phone, there's no account to create, and you pay once instead of subscribing. If privacy and a single payment matter to you more than cloud sync and recipe extras, that's the line that separates it, and the reason the app was built.
