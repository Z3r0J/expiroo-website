Looking for the best pantry inventory app to stop forgetting what's in the fridge? A handful of expiry trackers do the core job well; they mostly differ on price model, cloud versus local, and whether they want an account. Here's an honest, verifiable comparison — with the gaps marked rather than guessed.

## What to look for

The useful features are the same across the category: **barcode scanning** to add items fast, **best-before reminders**, and some way to organise **fridge, freezer and pantry**. The real differences are the **pricing model** (free, freemium, one-time or subscription) and whether the app is **cloud-based with an account** or **local and offline**.

## The apps, compared

| App | What it does | Price | Platforms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Expiroo** | Barcode, no account, offline | One-time purchase | Android |
| Fridgely | Barcode + receipt scanning, alerts, recipes, spaces, family, sync | Freemium (Premium adds CSV export) | iOS + Android |
| Pantry Check | Barcode, cloud inventory with sync, best-before reminders, lists, family sharing | Free to 200 items; Premium $11.99 / Pro $29.99 (as of July 2026) | iOS + Android |
| NoWaste | Fridge/freezer/pantry inventories, barcode + AI, sync | Freemium (Pro annual + Lifetime) | iOS + Android |
| Best Before | Per-item dates, barcode, optional account for sync | One-time (iOS) / free (Android) | iOS + Android |

A couple of honest caveats: for **Pantry Check**, whether it works fully offline or needs an account isn't confirmed — its cloud sync suggests an account, but we won't assert it. For **Fridgely**, only its "offline receipt scanner" is confirmed as offline.

## Where Expiroo fits

Most of these apps lean on the cloud, a login and a freemium or subscription upsell. [Expiroo](/en) takes the opposite trade-off on purpose: **no account, works offline, and a one-time purchase**. Your inventory lives in a local database on your phone. If you value privacy and paying once over cloud sync and recipe extras, that's the distinction that matters — and it's the reason Expiroo exists.
