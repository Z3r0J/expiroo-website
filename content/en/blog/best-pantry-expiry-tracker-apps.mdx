**The best food expiration tracker app for Android depends on one choice: a private list on one phone, or a shared inventory synced across your household.** For one phone with no account, look at Expiroo, Food Expiration Dates or the open-source Dispensa; for a family that shops together, Your Food and NoWaste are the strongest options on Google Play by installs and ratings.

All of them do the core job: you log what's in the fridge, freezer and pantry, and the app reminds you before it expires. Where they differ is the account, offline use, how much the free version covers, and whether the paid tier is a subscription or a one-time purchase. That's what this comparison is about.

A disclosure first: we make Expiroo, so weigh our take accordingly. Every competitor fact below comes from the app's Google Play listing or its developer's own website, checked on **November 19, 2026**. Where neither source says something, the table says "not stated" instead of guessing.

## Quick picks

- **Private, offline, no account:** [Expiroo](/en) (free up to 10 items, Premium is a one-time purchase) or Food Expiration Dates (open source).
- **Fully free and open source:** Dispensa, from F-Droid.
- **Household sharing on Android and iPhone:** Your Food or NoWaste.
- **Biggest free tier:** NoWaste (500 items) or Pantry Check (200 items).
- **Receipt scanning to add a whole shop at once:** NoWaste or FridgeWise.
- **A shop, café or stockroom:** BEEP, which is built for teams.

## The apps, one by one

### Expiroo

Our app. [Expiroo](/en) is a local-first expiry tracker: your inventory lives in a database on your phone, there's no account to create, and it never needs a connection. You scan a barcode to fill in the product name, add the date, and it reminds you 7, 3 and 1 days before and on the day itself (or switch to one daily summary). Storage locations are yours to define, and a waste report shows what you used against what you threw out.

The free version shows ads and tracks up to 10 items at a time. Premium is a one-time purchase, not a subscription: it removes ads, unlocks unlimited items and the full statistics dashboard. It's Android only (7.0 or later). An iPhone app called Expiroo exists, but it comes from a different developer.

**Pick it if** you want a private list on one phone and would rather pay once than subscribe. [Here's how to install and set it up](/en/blog/join-expiroo-beta).

### Your Food

The best-rated app here with a real track record: 50,000+ installs and 4.6 stars from 826 ratings on the US store. Your Food tracks food plus anything else in the house (medicine, cleaning supplies, pet food), with separate inventories for a second home, an office or a camper. Its site says the free version has no ads and includes personal sync across your own devices; Premium adds household sharing, larger inventories, sublists and budget tracking, sold as a monthly or yearly plan or a one-off lifetime purchase. It's on iPhone too.

**Pick it if** several people share one kitchen, or you also want to track non-food supplies.

### NoWaste

NoWaste keeps separate lists for the fridge, freezer and pantry and adds food by barcode, photo or receipt, with recipes, shopping lists and meal plans on top. It has the most generous free tier in this comparison: one location, five lists and 500 items, with no ads. Pro unlocks up to 20 locations, unlimited items, two reminders per item, a web app and household sharing. Its website lists Pro at $9.99 a year or $44.99 once (as of November 2026). It runs on Android and iPhone.

**Pick it if** you want meal planning and receipt scanning, and a large free tier.

### Best Before – Food Tracker

A long-running Android tracker (on Play since 2017, 50,000+ installs, 4.1 stars). It handles categories, storage locations, shopping lists and reminders both before expiry and after you open a product. The developer's FAQ says an account is optional and only needed to back up data online and share an inventory with your household. The free version shows ads; the listing shows one in-app purchase but doesn't say what it unlocks. Note that the iPhone app called "Best Before – Food Tracker" comes from a different developer.

**Pick it if** you want a mature, free app with opened-product tracking and don't mind ads.

### FridgeWise

A newer app (released July 2026) focused on getting a whole grocery run in fast: scan a receipt, snap a photo of the shopping, import a list or scan barcodes one by one. It also estimates freshness for items without a printed date and suggests recipes from what's about to expire. Its FAQ says you can use it as a guest, with an account needed to sync and share a household of up to six people. Basic caps the number of active items; Premium is a subscription. On Android and iPhone.

**Pick it if** you buy a lot of fresh food without printed dates and want receipts to do the typing.

### BEEP – Expiration Tracker

The most-installed app here (500,000+), but it's really built for shops, pharmacies and teams: scan, set the date, and invite staff by email. The free plan covers up to 50 products; Premium is listed at $4.90 per team per month on a 12-month plan (as of November 2026). Its US rating is 1.9 stars from 1,932 ratings, the lowest in this comparison. It's on iPhone too.

**Pick it if** you're tracking stock in a small business with several people.

### Pantry Check

Long established on iPhone, newer on Android (500+ installs). It scans barcodes against a crowd-sourced product database, reminds you before food expires, builds shopping lists from what you use and syncs in real time across a family. The listing says it tracks up to 200 items for free, with subscriptions for more.

**Pick it if** your household mixes iPhone and Android and you want automatic shopping lists.

### Food Expiration Dates

A simple, open-source tracker (MIT license, source on GitHub) that lists your food by expiry date with color cues, scans barcodes through Open Food Facts and tracks opening dates. Its website describes it as free and 100% offline. Pro adds early reminders up to 14 days ahead and a grocery list; the developer's September 2026 update lists it at €1.49 a month, €7.99 a year or €19.99 once (prices vary by country). Android only.

**Pick it if** you want a no-frills, open-source list that stays on your phone.

## Open-source options

Besides Food Expiration Dates, **Dispensa** is worth a look if you install apps from [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/en/packages/eu.frigo.dispensa/). It's free, MIT-licensed and ad-free, works offline with no mandatory account, scans barcodes (Open Food Facts) and can read the date off the label with the camera. Families can sync through their own Google Drive, WebDAV server (Nextcloud) or Syncthing instead of a company server. It needs Android 8.1 or later.

## Where Expiroo falls short

We'd rather you pick the right app than the wrong one with our name on it:

- **No sharing or sync.** Expiroo keeps everything on one phone. If two people need to see the same inventory, Your Food, NoWaste, Best Before, FridgeWise or Dispensa do that.
- **No iPhone version.** Your Food, NoWaste, FridgeWise, Pantry Check and BEEP run on both platforms.
- **A small free tier.** Ten items is enough to try the whole app, not to run a full pantry. NoWaste's free tier holds 500 items and Pantry Check's 200.
- **Ads in the free version.** Most apps in this list have none; Expiroo's go away with Premium.
- **No receipt scanning, recipes or shopping lists.** It does one job: dates and reminders.
- **It's brand new.** Expiroo launched on Google Play on November 18, 2026, so it has no public rating yet. Your Food and Best Before have years of reviews.

## How to choose

1. **One phone or a household?** Sharing narrows the field to apps with accounts or self-hosted sync.
2. **Would you rather pay once?** Expiroo, NoWaste and Your Food offer a one-time option; FridgeWise, Pantry Check and BEEP are subscriptions; Dispensa is free.
3. **How much do you track?** Count your pantry. If it's more than a few dozen items, the free tier matters more than the feature list.
4. **Does it need to work offline?** Only Expiroo, Food Expiration Dates and Dispensa state it outright.
5. **Will you scan or type?** All eight scan barcodes. Receipt scanning (NoWaste, FridgeWise) is faster for big shops.

Whatever you pick, the app only helps if you know what the dates mean. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a free app to track food expiration dates?

Yes. Dispensa (F-Droid) is fully free and open source, and Best Before is free with ads. NoWaste (500 items), Pantry Check (200 items), BEEP (50 products) and Expiroo (10 items) have free versions with a cap; Your Food and FridgeWise have free tiers whose limits are shown in the app.

### Which food expiry tracker works without an account?

Expiroo never asks for one. Food Expiration Dates and Dispensa don't require one either, Best Before only needs one for online backup and sharing, and FridgeWise has a guest mode.

### Can an app read the expiration date from the package?

Some can. Dispensa and the shopping-list app Pantrist read the printed date with the camera, and FridgeWise estimates freshness from the food type when there's no date. Most apps, Expiroo included, scan the barcode for the product name and let you enter the date.

### What's a good alternative to NoWaste or Best Before on Android?

If you want no account and a one-time purchase instead of a subscription, Expiroo is built for that. See our [side-by-side with NoWaste and Best Before](/en/alternatives).

### Is Expiroo on iPhone?

No. Expiroo is an Android app on Google Play. The iPhone app with the same name comes from a different developer.

## The short version

For a household that shares one fridge, Your Food and NoWaste are the strongest picks on Google Play today. For one phone, no account and no subscription, that's the gap [Expiroo](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.fusioncoreapps.expiroo) was built to fill, and Dispensa is the free, open-source route to the same idea.
