Expiroo is available on Google Play as a free download. This guide takes you from install to a working setup in a few minutes: get the app, add your first item with the barcode scanner, set up your storage locations and choose when reminders arrive. At the end you'll find what the free version covers and what Premium adds.

## What you need

An Android phone running **Android 7.0 or later** with the Google Play Store. That's it. Expiroo has no account and no sign-up, and it works offline, so there's nothing to register before you start.

One thing to know when you search: an iPhone app with the same name exists, but it comes from a different developer. The Expiroo in this guide is the Android app published by FusionCore Apps. Still deciding? See how it compares with [other food expiration tracker apps for Android](/en/blog/best-pantry-expiry-tracker-apps).

## Step 1 — Install Expiroo from Google Play

Open the [Expiroo listing on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.fusioncoreapps.expiroo) on your phone, or search the Play Store for "Expiroo: Food Expiration Date", then tap **Install**. Updates arrive through Google Play like any other app.

## Step 2 — First launch: no account, no cloud

Open the app and you can start right away. Your inventory lives in a local database on your phone: there's no login, no account and no server collecting what you eat, and you never need a connection to add or check an item.

If Android asks whether Expiroo may send notifications, allow it. Reminders are the whole point, and they can't reach you without that permission.

## Step 3 — Add your first item with the barcode scanner

Pick something from the fridge and add it. Point the camera at the barcode and Expiroo fills in the product name for you; add the expiration date and save. If Android asks for camera access, allow it so the scanner can work.

No barcode, as with fresh produce or leftovers? Choose **Enter manually** on the scanner screen and type the name yourself.

Your item now shows up on the home screen, which works like a traffic light: expired items first, then what expires soon, then everything that's still fresh. You can search any item by name or filter by where you keep it.

## Step 4 — Set up your storage locations

Expiroo starts with six default locations: **Fridge, Freezer, Pantry, Counter, Cabinet** and **Other**. If your kitchen is organized differently, tap **Add location** and create your own, such as a garage shelf, the cellar or the medicine cabinet. You can also pick a default location in **Settings**, so new items land where most of your food actually goes.

Food isn't the only thing you can track. Medicine, cosmetics and pet supplies work the same way: anything with a date printed on it.

## Step 5 — Choose your reminder schedule

Open **Settings** and look under **Notifications**:

- **Reminder intervals**: by default you get a reminder 7, 3 and 1 days before an item expires, and again on the day. Keep the ones you want.
- **Reminder time**: the time of day reminders arrive.
- **Daily summary**: one notification that names what's due, such as "3 items expire today or tomorrow". It isn't sent on days with nothing due.

The same screen also has the light and dark theme and the app language.

## Free vs Premium

The free version gives you the whole app, so you can try everything before deciding. Expiroo Premium is an optional upgrade you buy inside the app through Google Play.

| | Free | Premium |

| Items tracked | Up to 10 at a time | Unlimited |
| Ads | Yes | No |
| Barcode scanner, reminders, locations | Included | Included |
| Full statistics dashboard | No | Yes |
| Cost | Free | One-time purchase, no subscription |

Premium is a one-time purchase, not a subscription: pay once and keep it.

## Using it for a shop or café

Expiroo does the same job for near-expiry stock in a small shop, a café or a stockroom. Scan products in, sort them by date, and mark them down before they turn into a loss.

## Troubleshooting

- **Reminders don't arrive.** Check that notifications are allowed for Expiroo in your phone's settings, and that the reminder intervals you want are switched on under **Settings › Notifications**.
- **Google Play says the app isn't compatible with your device.** Expiroo needs Android 7.0 or later.
- **The scanner doesn't recognize a product.** Use **Enter manually** and type the name; the reminders work the same way.
