**Yogurt that has stayed covered in the fridge is usually good for 7 to 10 days past its "sell by" date, according to Clemson University's extension service; after that it gets noticeably sourer.** Houston Methodist, using the FoodKeeper storage guide, puts the outer limit at 1 to 2 weeks after you buy it, and throwing out what's left after that.

The printed date on yogurt is about quality, not safety. Except for infant formula, US manufacturers aren't required to put quality dates on food at all ([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/food/consumers/how-cut-food-waste-and-maintain-food-safety)). What decides whether yogurt is still fine is how it was stored, whether it's been opened, and what you see when you lift the lid.

## Short answer

| Situation | How long |
| --- | --- |
| Unopened, kept cold, past the sell-by date | About 7 to 10 days ([Clemson HGIC](https://hgic.clemson.edu/factsheet/safe-handling-of-milk-dairy-products/)) |
| Counting from the day you bought it | 1 to 2 weeks ([Houston Methodist](https://www.houstonmethodist.org/blog/articles/2022/dec/how-long-is-yogurt-good-after-its-expiration-date)) |
| Opened single cup | Within 24 hours, per one maker ([Stonyfield](https://www.stonyfield.com/faq)) |
| Opened large tub (quart) | Within 7 days, per the same maker ([Stonyfield](https://www.stonyfield.com/faq)) |
| Left out more than 2 hours | Throw it out ([Houston Methodist](https://www.houstonmethodist.org/blog/articles/2022/dec/how-long-is-yogurt-good-after-its-expiration-date)) |
| In the freezer | 1 to 2 months, though the texture changes ([Houston Methodist](https://www.houstonmethodist.org/blog/articles/2022/dec/how-long-is-yogurt-good-after-its-expiration-date)) |

Not every brand is that relaxed. Dannon says it can't recommend eating its yogurt past the "best if used by" date on the cup ([Dannon](https://www.dannon.com/faq/)). The windows above assume a fridge at 40°F (4°C) or below ([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/food/consumers/how-cut-food-waste-and-maintain-food-safety)).

How yogurt compares with milk, cheese and the rest of your fridge is in our [fridge storage chart](/en/blog/how-long-does-food-last-in-the-fridge).

## Unopened vs opened

**Unopened**, yogurt is sealed and its date assumes it stays that way. Kept cold, it typically lasts 7 to 10 days past the sell-by date. Past that point it develops a stronger taste ([Clemson HGIC](https://hgic.clemson.edu/factsheet/safe-handling-of-milk-dairy-products/)).

**Opened**, the clock speeds up. Stonyfield recommends finishing small cups and pouches within 24 hours of opening and quarts within 7 days, as long as they're refrigerated and tightly closed between uses ([Stonyfield](https://www.stonyfield.com/faq)). That's one brand's guidance, but none of the public-health sources we checked gives a separate window for opened yogurt, so it's a sensible yardstick.

A few habits keep an opened tub good for longer ([Houston Methodist](https://www.houstonmethodist.org/blog/articles/2022/dec/how-long-is-yogurt-good-after-its-expiration-date)):

- **Use a clean spoon every time.** Don't eat straight from the tub and put it back.
- **Reseal it tightly** right after serving.
- **Keep it on a shelf, not in the door,** so it stays at a safe temperature.
- **Put it back in the fridge promptly.** After more than two hours at room temperature, throw it out.

## Whey on top is normal

A layer of clear or yellowish liquid on top of the yogurt is whey, and it's normal. Yogurt maker Stonyfield says a little whey separates when the yogurt goes through a temperature change, gets squeezed, or is handled roughly. The company adds that it doesn't use artificial thickeners that might prevent this. Stir it back into a single cup, or pour it off a large tub ([Stonyfield](https://www.stonyfield.com/faq)).

Whey on its own isn't a spoilage sign. Liquid together with a curdled texture, a rancid smell or mold is a different story.

## Spoilage signs (bulging lid, mold: discard the whole cup)

Throw the yogurt out if you see any of these:

- **A bulging lid or container.** Iowa State University Extension lists bulging and leaking containers among the signs that food should be thrown out ([Iowa State](https://blogs.extension.iastate.edu/answerline/2025/01/21/inspect-before-you-toss/)).
- **Mold of any color.** Mold is on the same list. Discard the whole cup or tub, not just the spot you can see.
- **A curdled texture or rancid smell** ([Houston Methodist](https://www.houstonmethodist.org/blog/articles/2022/dec/how-long-is-yogurt-good-after-its-expiration-date)).
- **A noticeable change in color, consistency or texture.** The FDA says to check for exactly these signs on food past its "best if used by" date ([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/food/consumers/how-cut-food-waste-and-maintain-food-safety)).

Passing the look-and-smell check doesn't prove yogurt is safe. Bacteria usually don't change how food tastes, smells or looks ([Iowa State](https://blogs.extension.iastate.edu/answerline/2025/01/21/inspect-before-you-toss/)). That's why Houston Methodist recommends throwing out yogurt you haven't eaten within two weeks of buying it, even if it seems fine. Yogurt is made from pasteurized milk, but it can still be contaminated afterward, during processing and packaging.

People who should be strictest about this, per Houston Methodist: adults 65 and older, children under 5, pregnant women and people with weakened immune systems.

## Greek and fruit-on-the-bottom yogurt

**Greek yogurt** is regular yogurt strained to remove the liquid whey, which makes it thicker and tarter ([U.S. Dairy](https://www.usdairy.com/news-articles/what-is-greek-yogurt)). None of the sources we checked gives it a different fridge time from regular yogurt, so use the same windows: 7 to 10 days past the sell-by date unopened, and the opened-tub rules above.

**Fruit-on-the-bottom and sweetened yogurt** spoils faster once it's out of the fridge. Houston Methodist notes that yogurt with fruit or added sugars may spoil more quickly at room temperature, so get it back in the fridge fast.

## Freezing yogurt

You can freeze yogurt to stop the clock, but expect a different texture.

- **How long:** 1 to 2 months in the freezer. Thaw it in the fridge, not on the counter ([Houston Methodist](https://www.houstonmethodist.org/blog/articles/2022/dec/how-long-is-yogurt-good-after-its-expiration-date)).
- **The catch:** freezing changes the texture, and U.S. Dairy doesn't recommend it for that reason. It can also mean losing some of the live and active cultures, though the nutritional value isn't significantly affected ([U.S. Dairy](https://www.usdairy.com/news-articles/can-you-freeze-yogurt)). Clemson's extension service also advises against freezing because the results are so variable ([Clemson HGIC](https://hgic.clemson.edu/factsheet/safe-handling-of-milk-dairy-products/)), and Dannon doesn't recommend freezing its products ([Dannon](https://www.dannon.com/faq/)).
- **Plan ahead:** when a recipe calls for yogurt, allow time to thaw it in the fridge ([Houston Methodist](https://www.houstonmethodist.org/blog/articles/2022/dec/how-long-is-yogurt-good-after-its-expiration-date)). Stonyfield still recommends using frozen yogurt by the date stamped on the container ([Stonyfield](https://www.stonyfield.com/faq)).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you eat yogurt after the expiration date?

Usually, if it's unopened and has been kept cold: Clemson's extension service gives it about 7 to 10 days past the sell-by date. Check for mold, a bulging lid, a curdled texture or a rancid smell first, and throw it out if you see any of them.

### How long does yogurt last once opened?

There's no official figure. Stonyfield recommends 24 hours for small cups and 7 days for quarts, kept tightly closed in the fridge.

### Is the liquid on top of yogurt bad?

No. It's whey, which separates naturally. Stir it back in or pour it off.

### Can you eat yogurt with a little mold if you scoop it out?

No. Throw out the whole cup or tub.

### Can you freeze yogurt?

Yes, for 1 to 2 months, but the texture changes and some live cultures may be lost. Thaw it in the fridge.
